📚 History of the School

For decades, the Unidad Educativa Fiscal “Primicias de la Cultura de Quito” has been an important symbol of education, culture, and technical development in the south of Quito.

Traditionally known as Colegio Primicias, it is considered one of the most emblematic and historic public institutions in the city.

The school was founded in 1976. Its name pays tribute to the history of Ecuador and journalism, honoring “Primicias de la Cultura de Quito”, the first newspaper of the city and the country, founded and written by Dr. Eugenio de Santa Cruz y Espejo on January 5, 1792.

Inspired by the critical, scientific, and libertarian thinking of Eugenio Espejo, the institution was created with the mission of providing formal education to the growing population in the south of Quito.

Throughout the years, the school became an important reference for technical education in the sector, especially in Automotive Electromechanics and Informatics.

🏫 Conclusion

The Unidad Educativa Fiscal “Primicias de la Cultura de Quito” is more than an educational institution; it is a place full of history, culture, knowledge, and opportunities for thousands of students in southern Quito.

Since its foundation in 1976, the institution has maintained its commitment to academic excellence, technical education, and the personal growth of students. Throughout the years, the school has become a symbol of dedication, discipline, responsibility, and innovation.

Through its technical specializations in Informatics and Automotive Electromechanics, students develop practical abilities, technological knowledge, creativity, and professional skills that prepare them for the future and for the challenges of modern society.

In addition to academics, the institution promotes important values through cultural festivals, sports competitions, peace bands, artistic presentations, teamwork, leadership, and community participation.

Teachers, authorities, and students work together every day to create a positive environment based on respect, effort, solidarity, and responsibility. These experiences help students grow not only academically, but also personally and socially.

Today, “Primicias de la Cultura de Quito” continues inspiring new generations to achieve their dreams, discover their talents, and contribute positively to society.

Its history, traditions, academic excellence, and institutional identity make this school an important symbol of pride, culture, and educational development in the south of Quito.
